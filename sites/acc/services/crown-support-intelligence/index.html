Crown Support Intelligence (CSI)

A recurring review for in-house accounting teams: risk alerts, guidance on new tax rules and an expert reference line — not a substitute for day-to-day execution.

What this service covers

Crown Support Intelligence (CSI) is a recurring subscription for businesses with in-house accounting teams that want an additional layer of independent review and ongoing risk alerts. It is a direct line to Crown’s tax and accounting experts, not an outsourced accounting department. The principle is that CSI is a reference resource, never a substitute for day-to-day execution.

Value delivered

The service includes continuous review of business-provided reporting data, risk flags from a tax-authority perspective, explanations of newly issued tax rules for practical application, and access to a professional information network.

How we work

Crown discusses your needs, agrees the scope and timeline, performs the work and reports findings in line with the agreed engagement terms.

Frequently asked questions

Does CSI replace the in-house accounting team?

No. CSI is an expert reference resource and an additional review layer. It is not a substitute for day-to-day accounting execution.

What does the subscription include?

Recurring review of data the business provides, tax-risk alerts, explanations of new tax rules, and access to Crown’s expert reference line.

Discuss your needs

Contact Crown to agree a suitable scope. We do not reach conclusions before discussing your business’s situation.
