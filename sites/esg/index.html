ESG pressures facing businesses today

ESG now shapes orders and access to capital

Four ESG pressures directly affect competitiveness and access to business resources.

Pressure to meet export-market ESG requirements

Major buyers and export markets such as the EU and US increasingly require evidence of ESG, CBAM and supply chain responsibility; non-compliance risks lost orders.

Difficulty raising capital from professional investment funds

Without structured ESG reports, businesses may struggle to convince international investors and strategic partners during pre-investment due diligence.

Missing access to preferential low-interest financing

Banks increasingly prioritize green credit for businesses with clear ESG records; inadequate data and reports can mean missed preferential financing opportunities.

Pressure from Vietnam's Net Zero goals

Net Zero commitments are becoming greenhouse gas inventory requirements; businesses need a compliance roadmap now to stay prepared.

Current business context

ESG requirements businesses need to address

ESG requirements are extending from large groups to supply chains and SMEs.

Net Zero is becoming regulation

Net Zero roadmaps are translating into greenhouse gas inventory requirements for listed regulated businesses, directly affecting compliance activities.

EU CBAM enters its full application phase

CBAM directly affects exporters to the EU across several sectors; missing emissions data can weaken cost competitiveness.

Major buyers include ESG in supplier selection

Many multinationals require transparent ESG reporting when selecting and retaining suppliers.

International financial institutions prioritize ESG-aligned projects

International banks and funds increasingly link green credit and preferential rates to transparent, verifiable ESG records.

Listed businesses face sustainability disclosure pressure

Listed businesses need transparent sustainability information in annual reports to shareholders and investors.

ESG requires specialist technical and legal expertise

Emissions measurement and independent assurance require relevant expertise to ensure reports meet market requirements.
