ABOUT CROWN

International foundations. Local understanding. Lasting partnership.

Crown Audit & Advisory connects international standards with the realities of Vietnam, helping businesses pursue transparency, integration and sustainable growth.

Our origins

From colleagues to a shared ambition.

Crown was established by former auditors who worked together in audit and advisory at Deloitte Vietnam. Shared professional foundations and trust built through working together became Crown’s starting point.

We founded Crown with the ambition to deliver international-standard professional quality at a cost that makes sense for businesses in Vietnam. Local understanding helps us translate international experience into a practical, relevant approach.

Vision & Mission

Clear direction. Meaningful value.

Our vision

To become Vietnam’s leading platform helping businesses achieve efficient compliance, strong governance and access to capital at an optimal cost.

Our mission

To help businesses strengthen their international integration and competitiveness by connecting global standards with Vietnam’s advantages.

Our partnership

More than a service. A partner for the journey.

Crown works alongside clients to build transparency, management grounded in numbers and professional operations. From audit to finance, tax and sustainability, we connect specialist perspectives to support businesses more comprehensively.
