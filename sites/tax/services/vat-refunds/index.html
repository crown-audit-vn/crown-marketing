VAT refunds

Review VAT refund eligibility and standardize applications; reconcile evidence, prepare explanations and track requests for additional documents.

What this service covers

The work reviews VAT refund eligibility and standardizes applications, including reconciling evidence, preparing explanations and tracking requests for additional documents.

Value delivered

This can reduce repeated document requests and support processing timelines and cash flow planning.

How we work

Crown discusses your needs, agrees the scope and timeline, performs the work and reports findings in line with the agreed engagement terms.

Related services

Periodic tax risk reviewReview corporate income tax, VAT and personal income tax quarterly or annually; reconcile filings with books and documents to identify errors. Tax representation and dispute supportRepresent businesses before tax authorities, prepare arguments and evidence, and support dispute resolution within the agreed service scope. Frequently asked questions

What does the review include?

Eligibility for a VAT refund, standardization of the application, reconciliation of evidence and support with explanations or follow-up documents.

How does this help cash flow?

Clearer applications and fewer repeated document requests can support processing timelines and cash-flow planning.

Discuss your needs

Contact Crown to agree a suitable scope. We do not reach conclusions before discussing your business’s situation.
