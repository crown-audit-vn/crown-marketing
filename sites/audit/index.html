Common concerns

What does your business need from an audit partner?

Timing, file quality, unidentified risks and capital efficiency should be clarified from the start.

Concern about delays and operational disruption

Unclear plans and document lists lead to repeated requests, affecting reporting schedules and daily accounting work.

Difficulty assessing quality and fees

Understand scope, team capability and deliverables to select the right firm, rather than comparing fees alone.

Concern about risks being identified too late

Persistent data errors or control weaknesses leave businesses unprepared to explain matters to shareholders, funders or regulators.

Unclear operational and investment performance

Without independent assessment of results and resource use, leaders struggle to identify causes and improvement priorities.

Current business context

Why businesses operating in the Vietnamese market need a qualified, accessible audit partner

Financial transparency demands are rising in Vietnam, while Big4-calibre audit services remain unevenly available across localities.

Tax authorities and regulators strengthen post-review and compliance supervision

Growing post-review practices expose unprepared businesses to additional tax assessments and penalties.

Banks and investors increasingly request financial statements audited by reputable firms

Financial records lacking a credible audit may result in financing or investment being declined.

Laws increasingly extend mandatory audit coverage

FDI companies, loan-funded projects, credit institutions and other businesses face increasingly stringent mandatory audit requirements.

Businesses outside Hanoi and HCMC struggle to access Big4-calibre audit services

Travel costs and limited local personnel make it harder for large audit firms to serve provincial businesses.

Experienced CPA auditors are scarce

Businesses, especially in provincial areas, struggle to find auditors with sufficient expertise and practical experience.

Investors and strategic partners increasingly focus on capital efficiency

Beyond bookkeeping compliance, actual investment performance is becoming a key assessment criterion.

Our Process

Four steps of working with Crown Audit

Assess & plan the audit

Assess the business, define scope and key risks, and develop a suitable audit plan.

On-site audit work

Auditors work directly at the business, gather evidence and promptly discuss emerging issues.

Quality review & report issuance

Files and draft reports undergo quality review; matters requiring clarification are discussed before issuance.

Post-audit support

Discuss findings, improvement recommendations and further support consistent with the auditor's role.

Partnership opportunities for auditors

Partner with Crown Audit in the locality you know

For experienced auditors with local networks who want to develop professional work through a partnership with Crown.

Partnership principle: Crown retains the brand and quality-control role; most benefits belong to the local audit partner.
